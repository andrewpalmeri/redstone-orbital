BREAKING: White House nominates Lt. Gen. David Miller Jr. to lead U.S. Space Command

Space Force Lt. Gen. David N. Miller Jr. has been nominated to succeed Gen. Stephen Whiting as commander of U.S. Space Command. If confirmed, he would oversee the command's headquarters transition to Redstone Arsenal in Huntsville.

USSPACECOMSpaceForceRedstoneArsenalHuntsvilleLeadershipBREAKING

The White House has nominated Space Force Lt. Gen. David N. Miller Jr. to command U.S. Space Command, succeeding Gen. Stephen Whiting, who is expected to retire by January 2027, Air & Space Forces Magazine reported today.

A notice of Miller’s nomination for promotion to general appeared in the Congressional Record on Sept. 29 but did not list an assignment. U.S. Space Command and the Space Force both confirmed on Sept. 30 that Miller has been tapped for the SPACECOM job. Chief of Space Operations Gen. Douglas Schiess congratulated Miller on the nomination in a statement.

Miller is currently the Space Force’s deputy chief of space operations for strategy, plans, programs and requirements. A career space operator, he commanded Combat Forces Command, then known as Space Operations Command, from 2024 to 2025. Before that, he was director of operations, training and force development at U.S. Space Command.

If confirmed by the Senate, Miller would be SPACECOM’s fourth leader since the command was reestablished in 2019. Whiting has led the command since January 2024.

Why it matters for Huntsville: Miller would be the commander who oversees construction of SPACECOM’s first purpose-built headquarters at Redstone Arsenal and the relocation of personnel and operations from Peterson Space Force Base, Colorado. That phased transition is slated to wrap up in 2031. Just yesterday, Parsons won an $85 million owner’s-advisor deal to help manage the design and construction of the new campus. Miller’s tenure would cover the stretch when the Huntsville headquarters goes from plans to people in seats, which makes this nomination one for the Tennessee Valley to watch.
