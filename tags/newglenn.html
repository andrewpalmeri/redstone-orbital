Daily Brief Oct 26, 2026

NASA lays out the Artemis III flight plan and reroutes both Moon landers; Golden Dome's funding clock runs toward Dec. 11

At Friday's briefing NASA held Artemis III to summer 2027 and published a 14-day Earth-orbit plan to dock Orion with Blue Origin's and SpaceX's landers, dropped the halo-orbit rendezvous for both Artemis IV-era landers, and said it is working with ULA on an extended Centaur V to put Orion in low lunar orbit. Golden Dome still has no fiscal 2027 money, and the Space Force moved its first Next-Gen OPIR missile-warning satellite from Vulcan to Falcon Heavy.
Daily Brief Oct 19, 2026

NASA sets a Friday Artemis update as an Army interceptor warning and a Russian satellite's pursuit of ICEYE raise the stakes

NASA leadership briefs Oct. 23 on Artemis III and the 2028 landing goal, after Administrator Isaacman said Kennedy has cut its stacking schedule in half. JAXA's MMX launches today with NASA instruments and a Blue Origin-built sampler, and Blue Origin announced a $330 million New Glenn expansion. On missile defense, CBS reports the Army chief warned before the Iran war that THAAD and Patriot stocks would stay strategically low until 2030. Russia's Cosmos 2612 has also closed on ICEYE-X36, a U.S.-registered radar satellite tied to Ukraine.
Daily Brief Sep 30, 2026

New Glenn seeks 50 launches a year as the Senate puts Golden Dome's cost gap on the record

FAA opens comment on a Draft EA that would lift New Glenn's Cape cadence from 12 to 50 launches a year: a license ceiling, not a manifest. At SASC, CBO's $1.2T notional Golden Dome estimate met the Pentagon's $185B, and the testimony shows why they measure different things.
