NASA says Moon Base is ahead of schedule, and signs a nuclear pact to keep it running through the lunar night

At Thursday's Golden Age Summit, NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman said the first Moon Base element is planned to launch in December, with near-monthly uncrewed robotic missions from early 2027, beating the 2030 target.
