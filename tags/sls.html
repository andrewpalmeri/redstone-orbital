Artemis III's rocket is taking shape: SLS boosters stacked, all four engines on the core stage

NASA says both SLS solid rocket boosters are fully stacked on the mobile launcher inside the Vehicle Assembly Building and all four RS-25 engines are installed on the Artemis III core stage. Next comes a "short stack" rollout to Pad 39B for a tanking test and a crew launch-day rehearsal, while Marshall in Huntsville has finished welding the Artemis III spacer.
