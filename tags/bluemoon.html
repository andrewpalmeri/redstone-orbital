Daily Brief Nov 2, 2026

NASA won't commit SLS for Artemis III until Blue Moon is in orbit, as Google eyes a Golden Dome data role

Administrator Jared Isaacman told NASASpaceFlight that Artemis III will not commit the crewed SLS launch until Blue Origin's Blue Moon test lander is in orbit and checked out, naming the lander as the schedule driver. NASA opened a commercial-servicing RFI for Hubble, Swift reenters this week, and Starship Flight 15 still awaits its FAA license. Bloomberg reports Google is in early talks on a Golden Dome data-security group, and State cleared a $2.1 billion, 500-interceptor PAC-3 MSE sale to Ukraine paid for by allies.
Daily Brief Oct 30, 2026

NASA eyes June for Artemis III as the Army picks Lockheed's PAC-3 Edge for its Future Interceptor

Aviation Week reports NASA is aiming to launch the third SLS in June 2027, and Blue Origin expects to finish welding the Blue Moon Mk2 test article's hull by January. Starship Flight 15 moved to Nov. 12 on FAA planning data. The Army awarded Lockheed Martin a $1.08 billion Future Interceptor agreement for PAC-3 Edge, while Golden Dome waits on the Dec. 11 funding deadline.
Daily Brief Oct 26, 2026

NASA lays out the Artemis III flight plan and reroutes both Moon landers; Golden Dome's funding clock runs toward Dec. 11

At Friday's briefing NASA held Artemis III to summer 2027 and published a 14-day Earth-orbit plan to dock Orion with Blue Origin's and SpaceX's landers, dropped the halo-orbit rendezvous for both Artemis IV-era landers, and said it is working with ULA on an extended Centaur V to put Orion in low lunar orbit. Golden Dome still has no fiscal 2027 money, and the Space Force moved its first Next-Gen OPIR missile-warning satellite from Vulcan to Falcon Heavy.
