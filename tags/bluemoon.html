Daily Brief Oct 26, 2026

NASA lays out the Artemis III flight plan and reroutes both Moon landers; Golden Dome's funding clock runs toward Dec. 11

At Friday's briefing NASA held Artemis III to summer 2027 and published a 14-day Earth-orbit plan to dock Orion with Blue Origin's and SpaceX's landers, dropped the halo-orbit rendezvous for both Artemis IV-era landers, and said it is working with ULA on an extended Centaur V to put Orion in low lunar orbit. Golden Dome still has no fiscal 2027 money, and the Space Force moved its first Next-Gen OPIR missile-warning satellite from Vulcan to Falcon Heavy.
